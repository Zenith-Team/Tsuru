# Deprecation Notice

September 14, 2026

Thank you for using Tsuru. In 2026 [Zenith Team](https://github.com/Zenith-Team) released [Project RedHadron](https://prh.zenith.red): an advanced modding SDK for Wii U games, including NSMBU. Due to this recent development, Tsuru and Trailblazer are now deprecated and won't receive updates or official support. You can still download these projects for legacy purposes, and we recommend upgrading your work to [Project RedHadron](https://prh.zenith.red).

We appreciate your continued support!
